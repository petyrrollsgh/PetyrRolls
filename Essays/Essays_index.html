Essays

By Petyr

The state of the world is in sad shape from many different perspectives. I have given much thought to defending democracy here at home, climate change and the environment in general, the role of men in society and the travesty of materialism. As I try to sort through my feeling on this matter, I find it best to put these down in essay form as it forces me to examine carefully each though and organize them in a coherent manner. I intensely dislike the sound bite length of argument we have so grown used to with the advent of the internet. If you disagree with my essays feel free to construct you own rebuttal and send me a copy. You have my address.
